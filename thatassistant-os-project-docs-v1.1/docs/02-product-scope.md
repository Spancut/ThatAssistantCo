# Product Scope

## Seven core product pillars

### 1. Communication and Client Requests

- selected request intake
- message interpretation
- multiple-request separation
- missing-information detection
- scope and sensitivity flags
- acknowledgements, replies and updates
- communication-related follow-up

### 2. Calendar and Meetings

- working hours and scheduling preferences
- buffers and protected time
- attendee and time-zone information
- scheduling and rescheduling proposals
- recurring meeting records
- agendas and executive briefs
- meeting notes or transcript intake
- Meeting-to-Delivery

### 3. Projects and Delivery

- quick actions, tasks and projects
- owners, deadlines and milestones
- dependencies and blockers
- project risk
- completion criteria
- delivery evidence
- project and client-delivery reporting

### 4. Client Lifecycle

- client records and contacts
- onboarding
- access and document collection
- readiness to begin
- review and renewal dates
- offboarding
- client archiving
- Client Brain lifecycle

### 5. Systems and SOPs

- recording, transcript or notes to SOP
- process analysis
- steps, owners, systems and exceptions
- checklists
- approval and publication
- version history
- approved process knowledge in the Client Brain

### 6. Executive and Personal Assistance

- executive briefs
- personal-administration work
- important dates and commitments
- private preferences
- travel preparation
- itineraries
- event planning
- confidential-context handling

### 7. Follow-Up, Reporting and Weekly Visibility

- waiting states
- approvals and missing responses
- reminder rules and attempt limits
- completion checks
- escalations
- Decision and Exception Queue
- Weekly Client Update
- Weekly VA Operations Review

## First-release principle

The first product supports a useful foundational workflow across all seven pillars. It does not promise complete automation or deep integration in every area.

## Initially manual or approval-based

- email sending
- calendar event creation
- project-tool task creation
- CRM updates
- travel bookings
- supplier bookings
- external file publication
- client-facing report sending

## Specialist expansions

- Social Media Operations
- Finance Administration
- Agency Edition
- Advanced Integrations

## Future editions

- Founder Edition
- Agency Edition

Founder Edition will reuse the same operating foundation but orient workflows around one company, its clients, team, delegation and founder decisions.
